# Party, workspace and supplier accounting registers

| Register | Row identity | What the operator can decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Third parties](third-parties.md) | One party in a visible entity | Who is a customer, prospect or supplier under the selected categories, assigned representatives and contact criteria? |
| [Supplier invoice line account assignment](supplier-line-accounts.md) | One eligible supplier invoice detail | Which lines still need a purchase account, and which candidate does the chosen account filter actually search? |
| [Bookmarks](bookmarks.md) | One navigation entry | Which links are personal or shared and who may remove a selected link? |

The [membership subscription register](../specification/people-and-services/member-subscription-register.md) concerns dated membership periods and charges. A membership record, a billing party and a user account keep separate identities.
