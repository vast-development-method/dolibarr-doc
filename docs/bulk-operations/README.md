# Commercial list operations

A bulk instruction acts on a selected set of record identifiers. Its outcome depends on the operation. Issuing three invoices can be one transaction. Collecting payment for three invoices creates up to three independent payments and bank entries. Combining three invoice printouts creates a file. It does not consolidate their balances.

The scope is the ordinary commercial register for parties, sales offers, sales orders, dispatches, customer invoices, supplier price requests, purchase orders, receipts and supplier invoices. The action map records the choices offered by these nine lists. Additional record types, card-only actions, extension-supplied actions and alternative entry points have their own dispatch contracts.
