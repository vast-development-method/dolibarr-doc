# Commercial cost basis and margins

For a sale at 100 net before discount with a 10% line discount and a cost of 60, the realised selling price is 90 and the unit margin is 30. Dolibarr exposes two useful rates. The **margin on cost** is 30 / 60 = 50%. The **margin on sales** is 30 / 90 = 33⅓%. Margin reports use tax-exclusive sales. Document margins use the cost carried by the line, so the implementation must preserve the cost basis when it creates a sale. ## Selecting the cost

| Configuration or input | Cost selected by the corresponding operation |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit cost on a priced line | The unit-margin calculator uses the supplied cost. If the cost is empty and a specific supplier-price record is provided, it resolves that offer's unit price after supplier discount. Validate explicit zero independently when a supplier offer is also present. |
| Derive missing cost using selling price | When `ForceBuyingPriceIfNull` is positive and the input selling price is positive, the derivation method chooses the discounted selling unit price. This produces zero margin at that price. |
| Cost-price policy | A positive product cost price is preferred. Failing that, use a positive weighted-average price. If both are unavailable, try the supplier-price fallback. The module descriptor configures cost price as its initial policy. |
| Weighted-average policy | Use the product's positive weighted-average price. If missing, try the supplier-price fallback. |
| Supplier-price fallback | Seek the minimum supplier price applicable to the requested quantity. When no tier qualifies and quantity is positive, retry the lookup without a quantity threshold. If no price is found, the method can return zero. A missing required product or failed supplier lookup is an error. |

## Computation and reporting

**Unit calculation.** Round the discounted selling price at unit precision. For standard positive selling prices, subtract unit cost for the margin amount. A negative selling price uses the signed reversal of the margin on the absolute price. Divide by cost and selling price respectively for the two rates. The unit calculation leaves a rate undefined when its denominator is zero. The [unit result schema](../../schemas/commerce/unit-margin.schema.json) uses JSON null to represent this unset result. [Unit algorithm](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/margin/lib/margins.lib.php#L119-L161)

**Document summary.** Accumulate stored line net sales and quantity × signed unit cost. Cost follows the sign of the sales amount, including the zero-net credit-note case. The document groups products and services separately. Depending on configuration, an absolute discount belongs to products, services or only the document total. Compute each summary rate from its aggregated numerator and denominator. For these document summaries the code emits percentages only for positive denominators. Invoices billed by progress can scale cost by the progress percentage or its delta from the previous invoice, depending on situation and credit-note settings. [Aggregation and signs](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/core/class/html.formmargin.class.php#L64-L131) · [Discount handling and rates](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/core/class/html.formmargin.class.php#L132-L216)

**Customer report.** Read customer invoice details joined to the invoice and third party, including product and service lines, excluding draft and abandoned invoices and rows with a null buying price. Apply entity, date, product/category and salesperson visibility filters. Paid and still-outstanding validated invoices both contribute. A separately configured zero-cost filter can remove zero-priced costs. Filter scope and cost snapshots explain why a report can differ from the current catalogue margin. Related reports group by product or salesperson. The module distinguishes permission to view margins, permission to set margins and permission to read other users' margins. ## Acceptance cases

| Case | Inputs | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MRG-1 | Three units at 100, discount 10%, stored unit cost 60. | Revenue 270, cost 180, margin 90. Margin on cost 50%, margin on sales 33⅓%. |
| MRG-2 | Two units at net 50, explicitly free to acquire, with no supplier offer substitution. | Revenue 100, cost 0, margin 100. Margin on cost is undefined. Margin on sales is 100%. |
| MRG-3 | Credit all three units from MRG-1 at the same discount and cost. | Document revenue −270, signed cost −180, margin −90. Combined sale and credit have zero net sales, cost and margin. |
| MRG-4 | Product cost-price policy. Product cost price 0, average price 18, supplier price 16. | Cost derivation returns 18. Under the supplier-price policy, the matching offer determines the result. |
| MRG-5 | At report time an order exists for 270 and its only invoice is still draft. Validate the invoice and run the customer report again. | The first report excludes the draft. The second includes the eligible invoice lines regardless of whether cash has been collected. |
