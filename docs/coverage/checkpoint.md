## Worked outcomes checked in isolation

The invoice line calculator and invoice total updater reproduced all four [pricing cases](../../examples/finance/) with a temporary transaction store. At cent precision the two low-value lines produced tax of 0.02 when rounded individually and 0.01 when rounded by rate. For the [deposit allocation example](../invoice-processing/deposit-allocation.md) the line calculator returned gross values of 0.51 and 0.50 from unit amounts of 0.505 and 0.495.

On a controlled working-day calendar, the [leave counter](../employee-balances/month-end.md) started at 8.00 and recorded balances of 9.50, 8.50, 10.00 and 9.00 across two monthly periods. A repeat run kept four history rows. The full absence measured three days, while counting each month with the original boundary flags gave one day per period. These observations cover the isolated business functions with test data. They leave the user interface, provider callbacks and concurrent deployment behaviour to their own execution cases.

## Boundaries that require further evidence

The physical catalogue covers the declared setup tables and keys. Organisation-defined fields, an upgraded database, external providers and disabled features can produce a different installed model. For 21 table definitions the [operational record review](../records/operational-evidence.md) found no ordinary reader or writer in the examined business flows. Treat their declared fields and their business use as separate questions.

The operator register inventory covers its named set. Cards, specialist reports, message handlers, other per-record histories and organisation-configured operations can contain additional rules. The service catalogue establishes dispatch, arguments and documented decisions. Further work includes nested write variants beyond the billing examples, interacting settings and subscribers, transactions failing partway through a multi-document conversion, and concurrency during stock and journal posting. Provider delivery, authority acknowledgment and actual behaviour under competing transactions require execution evidence for their respective environments.
