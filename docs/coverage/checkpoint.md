## Decisions that change the answer

| Question | Trace and observable result |
| --- | --- |
| Can a balanced expense journal omit an employee liability? | [Expense preparation and transfer](../expense-accounting/worked-reconciliation.md). An unassigned zero account blocks the transfer. With an explicitly null binding, an eligible subset can produce debits and credits of 115 against claim gross of 161. Reconcile the posted control amount to the claim to find the 46 difference. |
| Which invoice amounts survive a change of source document or caller? | [Invoice processing](../invoice-processing/README.md) traces the 28 line-create and 23 line-amend inputs, conversion from orders and contracts, deposit distribution and account assignment. The [foreign-currency sale](../international/currency-from-quotation-to-settlement.md) separates face value, bank value and residual balance. |
| Which warehouse facts survive a correction? | [Production control](../production-control/README.md) connects the recipe, saved plan, partial and bulk posting, unit valuation and stock movement journal. A reversed serial can disappear from current actuals while both signed movements remain. [Lot and serial reconciliation](../production-control/lot-reconciliation.md) |
| Can employee balances be rebuilt from approved leave alone? | [Employee balances](../employee-balances/README.md) identifies the separate entitlement counter, posting policy, working-day conventions, monthly additions and leave deductions. Compare the counter and history before treating a split absence as fully charged. |
| Which records explain a number on a financial report? | [Customer and supplier payments](../registers-payments/customer-and-supplier-payments.md), [expense and salary disbursements](../expense-accounting/employee-payment-registers.md), [account preparation](../finance/supplier-account-preparation.md), [ledger selection](../registers-accounts/bookkeeping.md), and [financial statement rules](../specification/accounting/financial-statements-and-analytic-dimensions.md) distinguish documents, allocations, bank movements and posted legs. |

## Worked outcomes checked in isolation

The invoice line calculator and invoice total updater reproduced all four [pricing cases](../../examples/finance/) with a temporary transaction store. At cent precision the two low-value lines produced tax of 0.02 when rounded individually and 0.01 when rounded by rate. For the [deposit allocation example](../invoice-processing/deposit-allocation.md) the line calculator returned gross values of 0.51 and 0.50 from unit amounts of 0.505 and 0.495.

On a controlled working-day calendar, the [leave counter](../employee-balances/month-end.md) started at 8.00 and recorded balances of 9.50, 8.50, 10.00 and 9.00 across two monthly periods. A repeat run kept four history rows. The full absence measured three days, while counting each month with the original boundary flags gave one day per period.

## What changes the outcome of an operation?

| Implementation question | Rule, worked result and owning chapter |
| --- | --- |
| What do the 21 declared structures outside routine operational use contribute? | The [record-by-record review](../records/operational-evidence.md) identifies the active records for each workflow. Five ordinary operations ran with all 21 structures absent. For example, bank reconciliation changed the movement itself and the balance report grouped posted legs. |
| How do local fields, upgrades and disabled modules change what is stored? | A field definition and its physical value column have different lifetimes. Removing the last definition can drop values across owners. A disabled module can leave data in place, and an upgraded database can enforce keys absent from a fresh installation. [Field creation, removal and installed schema](../operating-boundaries/configured-model.md) |
| What does a write request do to each child record? | The [323-route input register](../service-writes/README.md) covers field assignment, selected members, individual arguments and structured children. A rejected middle quotation line rolled back its batch. A rejected second purchase dispatch left the first quantity posted. [Commercial documents and fulfilment](../service-writes/documents-and-fulfilment.md) · [Payments, assignments and files](../service-writes/allocations-and-relationships.md) |
| Which reads need a recovery decision? | Five GET handlers explicitly request persistent business changes or document generation. One worked ledger export returned an error after marking both selected legs. The ordinary retry then selected neither. [Read operations with side effects](../services/reads-with-effects.md) |
| Which subscriber executes, and what does it see after a failure? | [Priority and event selection](../event-effects/README.md) and the [setting combinations](../event-effects/setting-interactions.md) show how the shared object, activation, event and company policy select each handler. In execution, replaying an event project validation with two configured task labels requested four task inserts. A failing outbound target could also leave the next successful target with a negative recorded status. [Delivery, failures and replay](../event-effects/delivery-and-replay.md) |
| How much of a conversion survives failure? | The [conversion guide](../conversion-recovery/README.md) traces quotations, orders, contracts, shipments and receipts through creation, links, issue and output. In the [worked failure cases](../conversion-recovery/worked-failures.md), two issued payables and both primary receipt links survived, yet only one of two detail allocation links existed. The guide identifies which record to read before retrying each stage. |
| Can two successful receipts lose quantity or value? | With ten opening units, two overlapping receipts of three and four recorded both signed movements but left fourteen on hand under read committed. In another schedule all seventeen units were present while the cost was wrong. [Warehouse balances, lots, serials and valuation](../concurrent-posting/warehouse-and-lot-balances.md) |
| Can a balanced journal contain a duplicated invoice? | Two sessions posting the same invoice for 100 produced four legs and debits and credits of 200 each. Separate invoices could also share a piece number. Reconcile entity, source identity, accounts and amounts as well as debit and credit totals. [Concurrent journal posting](../concurrent-posting/journal-transfers.md) |
| Which operator view explains a displayed history or total? | Use the [record cards, panels and specialist reports](../operating-boundaries/record-history-and-reports.md) to identify the record owner, history feed and report population. A card's modification details, an agenda activity, a correspondence entry and a journal posting provide different evidence for the same transaction. |
| What happens when incoming mail reaches more than one business action? | The [inbound action guide](../communications/incoming-message-actions.md) follows 21 setup filters, an additional stored header filter, seven selectable actions and a runtime contact action. It explains ticket matching, per-message rollback and how the choice of mailbox adapter affects the final move. |
| What proves a document was sent? | A controlled relay accepted the same invoice attachment twice after the first acknowledgment was lost and the operator retried. The invoice recorded one successful send. The [delivery guide](../delivery-evidence/README.md) reconciles message identity, actual attachment, relay response, local history and final recipient evidence. |
| What proves a payment settled or a return was filed? | [Settlement and declarations](../operating-boundaries/settlement-and-declarations.md) traces checkout identifiers, allocation, transfers, generated return bytes and external evidence. A repeated payout can create another pair of bank movements. The bank confirms settlement and the receiving authority supplies filing acceptance or rejection. |

The examples state which operations ran with controlled collaborators, which used independent database connections, and which evidence belongs to an external party. When applying a worked case to a particular transaction, match its identities, settings and transaction boundary before comparing the result.
