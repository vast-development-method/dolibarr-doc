# What the blueprint covers

Release **24.0.3** is the functional baseline. The [worked trading cycle](../trade/worked-cycle.md) connects purchasing, dispatch, invoicing, collections and accounting. The tables below identify which parts of the release have a complete inventory and which business decisions still need specification. Full behavioral coverage remains open.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Continue from a business decision

A bounded next slice is expense-claim accounting preparation. Start at `htdocs/accountancy/expensereport/list.php:269–286` and follow an approved two-line claim with one expense account assigned and one still pending. Compare the line filter with the entries prepared in `htdocs/accountancy/journal/expensereportsjournal.php`. Determine which amounts follow the employee liability, the expense category and recoverable tax, which date controls the journal, and how a repeated transfer is recognized. The [posting chapter](../specification/accounting/source-journal-postings.md), [accounting record dictionary](../records/accounting.md) and [operator list index](../list-filters/screen-index.csv) give the starting records and report boundary.

The [detailed chapter directory](../specification/README.md) and [module map](guide-map.json) give equivalent entry points for other domains.
