# Transfer a prepared claim to the general ledger

## Selection and dates

1. Choose the expense journal, date interval and ledger-status filter. The initial query joins each detail to its claim and the claim to its employee. It selects the accounting entity's claims with `fk_statut > 0` and details with `fk_code_ventilation > 0`. A completely unbound claim has no candidate row. This positive-state test includes submitted, cancelled and refused states when bindings already exist. Normal manual preparation exposes only approved and closed claims.
2. The selected period and the configured accounting binding start test `expensereport.date_debut`, the start of the claim. The default accounting date `doc_date` is this same start. When `ACCOUNTANCY_ER_DATE_RECORD` is enabled, the stored journal date becomes `date_fin`. The selection still uses the start. A claim crossing two periods can be selected under one period and rejected on the posting date in the other.
3. The default “not yet posted” population excludes claims for which **any** bookkeeping row has `doc_type = expense_report` and `fk_doc = claim.rowid`. “Already posted” selects those claims. This membership test does not check that every expected expense or tax row exists. The accounting-period filter and current entity are applied to the source claims.
4. For the selected claims, a second pass counts **all** details with `fk_code_ventilation <= 0` and `total_ttc <> 0`. If it finds one, the entire claim is blocked. A zero-gross unbound line alone does not block. A nullable binding containing null satisfies neither the initial positive comparison nor this nonpositive test. Such a line can be silently omitted while its siblings transfer. The journal balances the selected accounts against each other and does not compare them to the header total. [Worked reconciliation](worked-reconciliation.md) gives an example.

## Derive a movement from the detail balances

The inputs are the stored `total_ht`, `total_tva`, `total_localtax1`, `total_localtax2` and `total_ttc` of each admitted detail. Unit prices, receipt dates, header gross and the parallel `multicurrency_*` columns are not operands in this transfer. A line created with inclusive pricing has already passed through quantity, price, tax and any applicable expense rules before accounting reads it. [Expense ceilings and mileage](../specification/people-and-services/expense-scales-and-ceilings.md) describes that earlier calculation.

| Row family | Group per claim | Positive amount | Negative amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee liability | Sum gross across admitted lines, grouped by employee subsidiary code or the configured employee account fallback. The general account actually written is always `ACCOUNTING_ACCOUNT_EXPENSEREPORT`. `subledger_account` receives the employee's own `user.accountancy_code`, which can be empty. `subledger_label` receives the employee's name. | Credit general control | Debit general control |
| Expense | Group by the general account number resolved from `fk_code_ventilation`. Sum the net for separate-tax accounting. With `ACCOUNTING_EXPENSEREPORT_DO_NOT_DISPATCH_TAXES` enabled, sum gross instead. | Debit expense | Credit expense |
| VAT | Under separate-tax accounting group `total_tva` by the purchasing account matched from `tva_tx` and optional `vat_src_code` using the organisation's tax context. Fall back to `ACCOUNTING_VAT_BUY_ACCOUNT`. | Debit input tax | Credit input tax |
| First and second local taxes | Accumulate each of `total_localtax1` and `total_localtax2` separately under the same purchasing-account selection as VAT. Write separate labelled rows for nonzero totals even when the account number equals the VAT account. Inclusive accounting suppresses all three tax families. | Debit tax | Credit tax |

Within each family, zero totals yield no booked row. For an ordinary claim, signed expense plus tax equals signed gross. The writer verifies total debits and credits at configured monetary precision. It attempts the liability first, then each resolved expense account, then VAT and each local tax. A missing expense account can leave its amount out of the attempted expense rows, causing an imbalance. An empty, undefined or sentinel general account, including the configured employee control, is rejected by the entry writer. Its general account validation is separate from the expense-account lookup and from fiscal-period validation.

## Identify and replay a movement

Each booked row stores `entity`, `doc_type = expense_report`, `fk_doc = expensereport.rowid`, `doc_ref = expensereport.ref`, `doc_date`, `numero_compte`, `label_compte`, `label_operation`, `sens`, `montant`, `debit`, `credit`, `code_journal`, `journal_label` and `fk_user_author`. The subsidiary fields are populated on the employee row. `fk_docdet` receives the **last detail encountered for this claim** in the input loop on every row. The only ordering in this read is by claim start. Multiple details for the same claim have no specified tie-break. Use the parent and its complete details to explain an aggregate. The single stored detail ID cannot allocate that aggregate back across receipts.

The writer looks for an existing piece using entity, source type, parent ID and source reference. It reuses that `piece_num` and ledger `ref`. Otherwise it allocates a new piece number from the entity's current maximum and obtains a new ledger reference. Document reference `doc_ref` and ledger reference `ref` refer to different records.

The transfer screen offers writing for the “not yet posted” selection once the control account is configured. The entry writer also checks entity, source type, parent ID, general account and operation label for a duplicate. It additionally compares the subsidiary when the incoming subsidiary is nonempty. An optional setting also compares the detail ID. Two configured profit/loss accounts bypass this duplicate test. Matching ordinary entries return `BookkeepingRecordAlreadyExists`. Amount, sign and journal are absent from the default duplicate comparison, while the operation label is mutable. This check operates on the proposed insert. A revised amount requires a separately authorised accounting correction.

Each claim runs in its own transaction. A blocked claim, invalid posting date, duplicate, failed insert or unbalanced totals rolls back that claim. Earlier successful claims stay committed. The batch checks the accumulated error count after rollback and stops when it reaches ten. On a subsequent default transfer, completed claims drop out and previously failed claims may re-enter. A pre-existing incomplete movement already satisfies the document-level presence test and needs explicit ledger reconciliation.

## Preview, export and cash are different records

The preview may display the bound subset of a blocked claim alongside its unbound-lines message. The optional draft CSV traverses the selected bound aggregates without skipping claims carrying this warning. It exports expense, VAT and employee gross. It omits the two local-tax arrays. Its employee account column uses the grouping key, which may be the subsidiary code. The posted movement has the configured general control and a separate subsidiary field. Use posted debit and credit rows to reconcile a final ledger export.

An actual reimbursement produces a separate payment record. With a bank posting, the bank-journal source is the bank movement, and the employee counterpart uses the expense-report control and employee subsidiary. The cash transaction and its ledger transfer can occur after the claim expense was booked, or before that accounting transfer. Match both independent events by their own references. [Employee payment registers](employee-payment-registers.md) explains why their periods and populations differ.
