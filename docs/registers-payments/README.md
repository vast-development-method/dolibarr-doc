# Reconciling cash, allocations and payment instructions

Before adding up a payment register, identify what one row represents. A bank account holds many movements. A single receipt may settle several invoices. A transfer instruction may still await collection, and a tax charge may be partly paid. The registers below select and total those records differently.

| Question | Register and unit of observation |
| --- | --- |
| How much cash is in each account, and which miscellaneous receipts and expenses are visible? | [Bank accounts and miscellaneous payments](bank-accounts-and-miscellaneous-cash.md). One account or one miscellaneous payment. |
| Which cheques were put into a deposit, and which collection or transfer instructions reference a particular obligation? | [Cheque deposits and transfer lines](deposits-and-transfer-lines.md). One deposit slip or one joined instruction line. |
| Which customer and supplier payments reference a party or amount? | [Customer and supplier payments](customer-and-supplier-payments.md). One payment and party group. |
| How do obligations, tax period ends and payments reconcile? | [VAT, local tax and social charges](tax-and-contribution-registers.md). One VAT obligation, local-tax record or social charge. |
| Which donations were recorded, and how much was actually received in the period? | [Donations and receipts](donations-and-receipts.md). One donation or one settlement against it. |

For payments to employees, see [reimbursement and salary payment registers](../expense-accounting/employee-payment-registers.md).
