# Chart selection, account assignment and reporting basis

A report total depends on both the accounting mode and the date of the source transaction. The simplified accounting module depends on customer invoicing and bank management. Its descriptor exposes the results-reading permission and declares a conflict with the double-entry accounting module. That second module supplies the chart, account bindings, journals and ledger. [Simplified module and right](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-e1dbf60f83e0) · [Double-entry setup](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-23769455c490)

## Financial report by customer or supplier

| Report basis | Source and time rule | Meaning of the result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoices due, `CREANCES-DETTES` | In the customer section, group invoices in states 1 or 2 by party, within accessible invoice entities and invoice date range. Include standard, replacement, credit and situation invoices. Deposit inclusion depends on the setting that treats deposits as payments. | Net and gross columns reflect the invoice amounts. A valid unpaid invoice contributes its full amount. |
| Cash in/out, `RECETTES-DEPENSES` | Join the payment via its invoice allocation to the invoice and party. Filter by payment date, then sum the amount on the allocation. | A payment split over invoices contributes the allocated portion to each party's group. Its cash contribution is shown gross. |
| Posted accounts, `BOOKKEEPING` | Join ledger account number and entity to a chart account in the selected chart version. Filter the entity and document date. The predefined result groups include `INCOME`, `OTHER_REVENUE`, `EXPENSE`, `COGS` and `OTHER_EXPENSES`. | The underlying signed aggregate is credit less debit. A posted sale can move to a different report period from both the issue date and collection date, depending on posting date. |

The report starts from the configured accounting basis, chooses bookkeeping as the default for double-entry accounting, then applies any explicit basis requested on the report screen. It restricts report access according to the active accounting module. On double entry it flags an explicitly requested operational basis. The date range, mode and selected accounting entity belong in the report contract. [Mode and access](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-80b4b23caf84) · [Basis warning](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-5e0d63220304) · [Ledger grouping](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-ee39c24ed82b) · [Operational selections](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-5e67193a8aa1)

**Worked acceptance case.** Issue a validated invoice for 100.00 net and 115.00 gross on 31 January. Allocate a 40.00 receipt on 3 February. With only this transaction, January's invoice-basis customer entry is 100.00 net and 115.00 gross. January shows no corresponding receipt. February's cash-basis entry is 40.00 and the remaining gross customer balance is 75.00. If the sales journal has yet to be transferred, a ledger-basis query cannot return a revenue posting for this invoice. ## Chart and accounts

A chart model has a version/code, country, label and active flag. A ledger account has a technical ID, entity, chart version, account number, labels, account type, optional parent account, optional reporting category, active flag and accounting options such as reconciliation. The unique key is account number, entity and chart version together. An account number on a report carries its chart and entity context. [Chart model](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-c166968c96e7) · [Account fields](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-2f9af49e3ee5) · [Account identity](../reference/locators/additional-domains.md#ref-abe860129e91)

| Command | Behaviour to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Select and load chart | The chart-management screen resolves the model's country, looks for the supplied account seed, loads it with entity-aware IDs and persists the chosen chart ID for that entity. When switching away from a used chart, the UI counts bound customer invoice, supplier invoice and expense lines plus entity bookkeeping rows and asks for confirmation. Existing bindings and posted records are not migrated by this command. |
| Maintain chart or defaults | Chart management has a distinct permission from operational data entry. Product account binding has its own accounting binding-write permission. The defaults page stores product/service, customer/supplier and other account settings used by the respective source flows. |
| Suggest an account for an invoice line | Calculate the generic domestic, intra-community or export default for product/service and customer/supplier direction. Look for the corresponding item-level mapping. When enabled and supplied, the third-party product-account override can displace the item suggestion. A configured deposit account can override those suggestions on deposit invoices, deposit lines and credit notes against deposits. Only fall back to a generic suggestion when eligible and generic auto-fill is enabled. Posting separately consumes the resulting line binding. |
| Retire or delete account | The usage check looks for customer invoice, supplier invoice and expense report detail records that reference its technical ID. A match blocks normal deletion and deactivation. This check specifically tests these three sources. |

**Acceptance.** Enter the same nominal account number twice in the same chart and entity and expect a unique-key failure. Reuse the number in a different chart or entity and resolve by the full key. Bind an active account to one supplier invoice line and request deletion. The used account must remain. For a domestic invoice with a generic sales default and a mapped item, enable the third-party override and supply its account. The party account should be suggested. With a configured, resolvable deposit account and a deposit document, the deposit account takes precedence. Reopening the suggestion screen by itself creates no new ledger entry.
