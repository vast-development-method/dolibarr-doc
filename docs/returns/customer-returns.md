# Customer returns, shipment cancellation and refunds

A customer who sends back two of ten delivered units has still received the original ten. Keep the original dispatch as the outbound record. Record the quantity accepted back at the receiving warehouse, issue a credit for the agreed amount, and track how the customer receives that value. If dispatch was recorded in error and the goods never left, Dolibarr offers a separate shipment cancellation action. [Shipment guide](https://wiki.dolibarr.org/index.php/Module_Shipments) · [Outbound dispatch and billing](../sales/dispatch-to-invoice.md)

## Correcting a shipment recorded in error

| Starting point | Operation and authority | Observable effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validated shipment, stock issued at validation | The shipment card offers **Cancel** to a user with shipment-create permission. Cancellation checks for linked deliveries and runs `SHIPPING_CANCEL`. | With Stock enabled, receive eligible quantities back at the warehouse on each original allocation. Reverse lots individually. The cancellation passes zero valuation price to preserve the weighted average. Kit-parent and component stock settings affect the movements. |
| Validated shipment, stock issued only at closing | Cancel before closing. | There was no issue at validation. Remove the shipment rows without an incoming movement. |
| Closed shipment, stock issued only at closing | **Reopen** with shipment-create permission. The database transition must match closed status. The card then allows cancellation of the validated shipment. | Reopening retains the allocations and reverses positive quantities at original warehouse and lot. The movement identifies the shipment as origin. A repeated reopen of an already validated shipment returns no work. A subsequent standard cancel removes the allocations without a second receipt under this stock policy. |
| Shipment with a linked delivery | Attempt cancellation. | The linked-delivery guard rejects the operation before the trigger or any reversal. |
| Order was in shipment-in-process | On successful cancellation the routine reloads quantities from remaining shipment rows. | If the result is empty, it requests a transition to validated. Even a remaining draft allocation can keep the order in process. A previously delivered order falls outside this reset condition. Read back the actual order status. |

**What survives cancellation.** The shipment header survives. Its line records, children and batch allocations are removed. The stock-reversal label includes the shipment reference, but the cancellation code deliberately clears movement origin. Preserve allocation information before cancelling if a reconciliation depends on source line IDs. Delete is a different command with a separate permission. For a direct domain cancellation of a closed shipment under stock-on-close, the stock reversal also requires an explicit stock-update argument. The ordinary card offers Cancel on validated shipments. [Line removal and reversal labels](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/expedition/class/expedition.class.php#L1824-L1892) · [Cancel and delete handlers](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/expedition/card.php#L674-L689)

**Failure after database commit.** The cancellation routine commits removal and stock work before deleting generated files. A file or directory removal failure returns zero. The card calls the separate status update to cancelled only after a positive return. A validated-looking header can therefore coexist with removed allocations and restored stock. Reconcile the header, originating order, remaining rows and individual stock movements before issuing another correction. ## Receiving goods and issuing a credit

| Decision | Stable operation | Record to reconcile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which shipment actually left? | Identify the shipment allocation, order line, SKU, original warehouse and shipped lot. Identify the warehouse and lot where the return was accepted. | The order and original shipment continue to show what went out. A return receipt accounts for what came back. [Stock ledger](../supply/stock.md) |
| How much commercial value is due? | Create a customer credit note (invoice type 2) for the party and normally select the original invoice. The card requires a date and checks the permitted future window. Standalone credits require `INVOICE_CREDIT_NOTE_STANDALONE` when no source is supplied. | The new credit note stores its own ID, dates, party, and source invoice ID. It copies the source terminal/module identifiers. For each linked object type on the source, the creation handler takes the first linked ID, so a copy of the generic links can be incomplete when several shipments fed one invoice. |
| Which lines are being credited? | On an ordinary goods invoice, copying lines reverses the selling price, tax and signed totals, preserves the quantity and leaves the purchase cost sign intact. Edit the draft quantity or select the appropriate lines for a partial credit. An amount-only reduction can use a free line. | For a two-unit return the credit has quantity 2. The customer and stock views use the same positive quantity, while the sales amounts are negative. |
| Is this a credit for the entire unpaid balance? | The optional remaining-amount action creates a gross-value line based on the absolute value of source gross less cash, applied credits and deposits. VAT follows the configured remaining-balance policy. | This calculation answers a balance question. A fully paid original invoice can still support a credit for goods returned. Use the goods lines to value that return. |
| Which operation receives the goods? | With Stock enabled, `STOCK_CALCULATE_ON_BILL` enabled and a positive validation warehouse, the credit-note validation routine calls stock receipt for each catalogued product line using its quantity, line batch, selected warehouse and price zero. | The receipt points to the credit-note ID. A free amount-only line has no catalogue product to receive. A financial concession on stock-tracked goods needs a choice of credit lines and warehouse consistent with actual physical receipt. |
| How are physical returns recorded under shipment-timed stock? | Use the stock movement operation for the accepted quantity if no credit-validation receipt covers it. Supply product, receiving warehouse, positive quantity and lot where applicable. An integration can supply a movement code, label and typed origin for reconciliation. Stock-create permission is required. | The manual stock service returns a movement ID and records the positive quantity as a stock increase with the default type. Count each physical unit once, including when the credit is processed later. |

## Paying back the customer or using credit on another invoice

**Cash repayment.** The card offers payment back on a validated, unpaid credit with a nonzero remainder and the invoice-payment right. A payment allocation against the credit note is stored negative. With banking enabled, select the bank account and reconcile the outgoing movement with the payment and allocation. The payment form accepts a selected amount. The distributed REST operation checks invoice visibility, compares a requested magnitude against the remaining amount by default, and signs the allocation for a credit note. Payment and bank posting are separate from shipment and warehouse updates. [Payment-back eligibility](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/compta/facture/card.php#L6872-L6894) · [Interactive refund](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/compta/paiement.php#L245-L323) · [Distributed payment](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/compta/facture/class/api_invoices.class.php#L1890-L2009)

**Partial-refund calculation in 24.0.3.** For a credit of −103.50 with a prior cash allocation of −34.50 and no applied credits or deposits, the remaining obligation is 69.00. The distributed endpoint computes signed gross **minus** signed cash and obtains −69.00. The single-invoice `POST /invoices/{id}/payments` computes the credit branch with signed gross **plus** signed cash, which yields a company-currency request of −138.00 before the payment service processes it. Its foreign-currency branch also starts from the full foreign total. Use the distributed endpoint with an explicit magnitude or its remaining-balance mode for this case. [Signed payment sum](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/core/class/commoninvoice.class.php#L342-L380) · [Single-invoice calculation](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/compta/facture/class/api_invoices.class.php#L1793-L1818) · [Distributed calculation](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/compta/facture/class/api_invoices.class.php#L1932-L1969)

**Reusable credit.** On a validated, unpaid, negative-net credit note, an invoice creator can convert the credit into available customer adjustments. The ordinary card requires zero prior cash unless `INVOICE_ALLOW_REUSE_OF_CREDIT_WHEN_PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` is enabled. The handler checks for an existing conversion, groups eligible lines by VAT rate and VAT source code, stores positive reversal components on adjustments referencing the customer and source credit note, and marks the source credit as paid on success. For a partially refunded credit, it scales each group by the absolute value of (credit gross minus signed cash already allocated) divided by credit gross, at configured unit precision. This closes the credit by assigning its remaining value to the customer without a new bank movement. `POST /invoices/{id}/usecreditnote/{discountid}` consumes an available adjustment against the target invoice's settlement. It checks invoice-create permission and visibility of the target invoice. The adjustment's source remains the credit note and its target becomes the invoice receiving the credit. A further generic link from credit note to target invoice is best-effort. When applying an adjustment, verify customer identity, availability and target balance, then read the adjustment's target and recompute that invoice's open amount. ## Acceptance cases

Assume one currency, two-decimal totals, stock initially 20, and ten units sold at 50 each with 10% discount and 15% VAT. The original sale is 450.00 net, 67.50 VAT and 517.50 gross. Cases beginning after delivery start with stock of 10. Each row is an independent case.

| Case | Given and action | Expected records and balances |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RET-01 | A validated shipment of ten was entered in error. It is the only allocation on an in-process order. Stock was issued at validation, with six of lot A and four of lot B. Cancel it. | Stock returns to 20 with +6 to A and +4 to B in the outbound warehouse. Shipment header becomes cancelled after successful status update. Details disappear. The order can return to validated. No customer credit or bank posting is created. |
| RET-02 | Stock is issued only on close. Reopen a closed ten-unit shipment entered in error, then cancel through the card. | Reopen receives ten and retains allocations. Cancellation removes allocations. Total quantity received across the two actions is ten. If the order was previously delivered, read back that status independently. |
| RET-03 | Customer paid in full, and now returns two of the ten delivered units. Stock is shipment-timed. Warehouse accepts both into lot A. Credit those two units. | A single +2 entry leaves stock at 12. Original shipment remains ten. New credit note carries −90.00 net, −13.50 VAT, −103.50 gross and a reference to the original invoice. No cash has been paid back at credit issuance. |
| RET-04 | Same delivered sale under invoice-timed stock. Validate the two-unit product credit with the receiving warehouse and lot A. | Validation itself creates the +2. Stock is 12. The original sales invoice and original shipment retain their quantities. |
| RET-05 | After RET-03 or RET-04, settle the entire credit by cash from bank. | One −103.50 credit-note allocation, and an outgoing 103.50 bank transaction. Stock stays at 12. Reconciliation of the bank movement is a later step. |
| RET-06 | Customer chooses future credit. Convert all of the −103.50 credit, then apply it to another invoice of 150.00 gross that has no earlier settlements. | Available adjustment records 90.00 net plus 13.50 VAT. Application consumes 103.50 and leaves 46.50 payable on the target. There is no refund payment or bank movement. |
| RET-07 | A prior refund of 34.50 was allocated as −34.50. Enable partial credit reuse and convert the balance. | Ratio is 2/3. The adjustment contains 60.00 net, 9.00 VAT, 69.00 gross. Cash paid plus reusable value is 103.50. With partial reuse disabled, the ordinary card hides this conversion action. |
| RET-08 | Customer receives a financial concession for late delivery and keeps all ten units. Credit the agreed amount on a free line, with no manual stock receipt. | Stock stays at 10 even under invoice-timed stock. The monetary credit can be refunded or applied to a later invoice independently. |
