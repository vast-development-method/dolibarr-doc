# Membership renewals and donations

Member dues buy a period of membership. A donation records a pledge and any receipts against it. A single payer may appear in both workflows. Keep the period, pledge, payment and bank transaction as separate business records. The release descriptors identify Membership as 310 and Donations as 700. ## Membership and period coverage

| Record | Meaning and relationships |
| --- | --- |
| Member type | Defines whether subscriptions are required and supplies the default duration value and unit for the renewal form. |
| Member | A person or legal entity with membership state, type, optional third-party/user linkage and derived coverage end. States are draft (−1), validated (1), resigned (0) and excluded (−2). |
| Subscription | One member, type at booking, amount, inclusive start/end dates, creation details, notes and optional bank transaction. Missing type at creation defaults to the member's type. |
| Customer invoice and payment | Optional accounting records generated by the selected renewal workflow. A direct-bank renewal and an invoiced renewal leave different evidence. |

When the renewal form receives no end date, it adds the member type's duration to the start, then subtracts one day. An unspecified duration defaults to one year. The underlying subscription creation rejects an end instant less than or equal to the start. The member's coverage end is the maximum end date across all retained subscriptions. The last subscription details are selected by latest **start** date. Deleting the final subscription clears the member's cached end date. A validated member can show as expired without changing the member's validation state. The display takes both the membership state's value and the type's subscription requirement into account. ## Record a renewal

The operator action requires `adherent.cotisation.creer`. It records the subscription and performs the chosen financial actions within the form's enclosing transaction. The default mode is subscription only. For a member type requiring subscriptions the amount must be numeric. In bank-recording modes the form requires a payment date. With banking enabled and a nonzero amount, the applicable fields include the payment label, method and account. A zero-amount subscription can record coverage. Supplying a bank account for a zero amount in the checking branch raises a form error. [Form checks and transaction](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/adherents/subscription.php#L236-L360) · [Commit and rollback](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/adherents/subscription.php#L455-L469)

| Chosen mode | Effects of a successful renewal |
| --- | --- |
| Subscription only (`none`) | Creates the period and refreshes coverage. |
| Direct to bank (`bankdirect`) | Credits the selected account, links the bank entry to the subscription and stores its bank row ID. |
| Invoice only (`invoiceonly`) | Resolves the linked customer, creates a standard invoice and a single service line for the dues, then validates it. This mode leaves payment outstanding. |
| Bank through invoice (`bankviainvoice`) | Performs invoice creation and validation, allocates the receipt to the invoice, records the bank transaction, writes its ID to the subscription and marks the invoice paid. |

The financial operation can be invoked with explicit permission to create a missing third party. The ordinary form call leaves that option at its default. The invoice line takes the subscription amount as **tax inclusive**, quantity 1. The configured subscription service and VAT setting determine the line's accounting detail. For an illustrative configured rate of 15%, dues of 115 result in net 100 and tax 15. Recording the invoice-only option cannot by itself demonstrate that 115 reached the bank. [Bank and customer preparation](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/adherents/class/adherent.class.php#L2002-L2126) · [VAT, invoicing and bank payment](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/adherents/class/adherent.class.php#L2128-L2247)

The subscription REST create operation accepts `fk_adherent`, `dateh`, `datef` and `amount`. It checks subscription creation permission and access to the member and returns a subscription ID. Its explicit call creates the period and refreshes membership coverage. The additional invoice/bank work described above belongs to the operator orchestration. ## Pledge, receive and close a donation

A donation has its own amount, status, donor details, optional third party/project and public/private information. A donation payment has one donation ID, payment date, amount, method, reference and optional bank row. A pledge may therefore have several receipts. A bank movement links to the donation payment. The donation payment table is distinct from a customer invoice payment allocation. [Pledge record](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/install/mysql/tables/llx_don-don.sql) · [Donation payment record](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/install/mysql/tables/llx_payment_donation.sql) · [Bank linkage](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr/blob/5c6c7ff6a5112413b1c27221c8d11750afad56f8/htdocs/don/class/paymentdonation.class.php#L611-L677)

| Action | Rule and result |
| --- | --- |
| Validate a pledge | Draft 0 becomes validated 1. The card separates donation read, create/edit and delete permissions. The REST validation operation checks the donation creation right and resource access. |
| Receive an instalment | The receipt screen requires donation create permission, the payment date, method and a bank account if banking is enabled. It creates a payment and then requests the bank posting in the same enclosing transaction. |
| Determine the outstanding pledge | Subtract the sum of signed donation payments from the pledge amount. The form offers the remaining amount as its suggested next payment. A rejected collection can have a negative payment value. A zero sum is refused by payment creation. |
| Classify as paid | The donation card offers this action when a validated, unpaid pledge has zero remaining. It updates the paid flag and status to paid (2). The receipt entry and classification are distinct actions on this operator path. |
| Cancel or reopen | The card offers cancel on draft/validated unpaid donations with zero received. Canceled is −1. It offers reopening a paid donation. State changes alone do not create an instalment. |

**Acceptance cases**

| Given | When | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A membership runs from 1 January through 31 December and a later short subscription ends 31 August | Save both | Latest-starting subscription is the short one. Membership coverage still ends 31 December. |
| A one-year type starts 1 November 2026 | Leave the end date blank | The renewal form derives 31 October 2027. An explicit end equal to the start is refused by the model. |
| A member has no linked customer and the ordinary form requests invoice only | Submit | The linked-customer step fails and the form rolls back the period with the financial action. |
| A validated pledge of 500 has receipts of 125 and 200 | View outstanding and post the final 175 | The outstanding amount goes 175 → 0. The card offers classification as paid. |
| The selected bank refuses the donation posting | Submit an otherwise valid instalment | The receipt form rolls back its database work. A separately collected external payment requires reconciliation against its provider reference. |
